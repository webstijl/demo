Who We Are

Murami Consulting is a renovation contractor based in Hilversum, in Het Gooi. We work throughout the provinces of Noord-Holland, Utrecht and Flevoland, including Amsterdam, Amstelveen, Amersfoort, Almere and the city of Utrecht.

What does Murami Consulting do?

We handle full-house, bathroom, kitchen and exterior renovations for homeowners, expats and international clients, in English or Dutch. One in-house team runs your project from planning to handover.

You get a fixed quotation before work starts, and any change to the scope is agreed in writing first. Every project is covered by CAR and business liability insurance up to €500,000, with a 1-year guarantee on workmanship. Most bathroom renovations take 3 to 5 weeks.

Guarantee & coverage

Fully Insured Renovations

Your home is one of your most valuable assets, and we treat it with the respect it deserves.

Every project we undertake is covered by a Construction All Risk (CAR) insurance policy, protecting your property throughout all phases of the renovation. In addition, we carry a comprehensive liability insurance (Aansprakelijkheidsverzekering), ensuring full coverage against unforeseen risks or third-party damages.

1 Year Guarantee

This insurance coverage complements our 1-year no-questions-asked guarantee on workmanship and materials. If anything goes wrong within the first year, we fix it — promptly and professionally.

€500,000 Insurance Coverage

Our insurance coverage extends up to €500,000, giving you complete peace of mind during the entire renovation process. In the unlikely event of unexpected circumstances, you are fully protected — financially and legally.

A complete bathroom renovation with Murami normally starts around €13,000 including VAT for a compact bathroom with a clear layout. The total depends on demolition, plumbing changes, tile size and pattern, niches, heating, ventilation and sanitary choices.

A full-house renovation varies considerably, so an early planning range is approximately €800–€1,500 or more per square metre including VAT. The final price depends on the size and condition of the property, structural work, electrical and plumbing upgrades, bathrooms, kitchen scope and finish level. After an on-site visit and detailed room-by-room scope, we provide a fixed quotation for the agreed work.

A complete bathroom renovation normally takes 3–5 weeks, kitchen renovation work approximately 2–4 weeks and a full-house renovation approximately 2–6 months. The exact programme depends on the scope, layout changes, access and requested changes. Planned start and completion dates are included in our quotation.

Yes. We work in English and Dutch and regularly carry out renovations for expats and international homeowners. We explain quotations, planning, Dutch renovation practices, permit responsibilities, material choices and project changes in clear English.

We provide a fixed quotation for the agreed written scope, rather than an open-ended estimate (excluding personal items of personal choice). If you request a change or we uncover a condition that requires work outside the agreed scope, we document it and explain the price and planning impact before proceeding. Extra work is only carried out after written approval, and the original quotation clearly identifies assumptions, exclusions and any provisional amounts.

Many internal bathroom and kitchen renovations do not require a permit, but structural changes, extensions, dormers, facade changes, monument work and some exterior projects may require an omgevingsvergunning, notification or technical documentation. We identify the likely requirement early and can help coordinate drawings, engineering and the application when this is included in our scope. Municipal fees and external-consultant costs are managed separately, and required approvals must be in place before work starts.

Yes. Murami Consulting carries Construction All Risk insurance and business liability insurance, with cover up to €500,000 subject to the policy terms. CAR insurance covers insured accidental damage to the project during construction, while liability insurance covers insured damage to clients, neighbours or other third parties caused by our work. Proof of insurance is available on request.

We provide a one-year guarantee on workmanship carried out by Murami, starting from the date of handover. Products and materials are covered by the applicable manufacturer or supplier warranty.
